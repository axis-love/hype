The gap between "hot take that gets likes" (11 likes, 0 comments) and "controversial take that starts arguments" (60 likes, 17 comments) is enormous. The difference: people on *both sides* of a real debate will reply. "Vibecoding is bad" is one-sided. "Manual coding should die" is two-sided.

Her 3 best-performing posts went outside it: model nationalism, gaming economics, coding culture war. When she stays inside ("I refactored my Cursor setup," "langgraph state management"), the audience is tiny and already agrees with her.

"Unpaid training data." "Death wish." These endings *imply* a position but don't *ask* for a response. Add: "convince me otherwise," "am I wrong?," or a direct question. The gatekeeping post got 17 comments because it was inherently debatable — not all topics are, but the framing can make them so.
